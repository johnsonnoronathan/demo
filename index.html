How a lesson works

  1. 1Watch

    A narrated video of a few minutes, with captions, chapters and adjustable speed.

  2. 2Read

    The same lesson in writing, paced for reading, with animations you can step through.

  3. 3Do

    A hands-on exercise on a practice app, with screenshots of what you should see.

  4. 4Delegate

    Copy the exercise's agent prompt into Claude Code or another coding agent.
