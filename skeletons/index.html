Night trains are back, and on most nights every berth is taken

Sleeper routes across Europe sold out most summer nights this year. Operators are now hunting for carriages.

LVBy Lena Vogel · 7 min read · 12 Oct 2026

The 21:04 from Vienna to Hamburg left with every couchette taken on 61 of 62 nights this summer. Its operator added a second set of coaches in July and sold those out within a week. A third set is on order for 2027.

Demand is not the problem. Most sleeper cars in service were built in the 1980s, and a new one takes about three years from order to track. Until the new fleets arrive, every extra departure runs on borrowed carriages.

Patterns adapted from transitions.dev by Jakub Antalik: skeleton loader and reveal, texts reveal, shimmer text and UI generation. Durations, distances, blurs and easings follow its motion tokens.

This page is a fresh, dependency-free implementation in CSS and vanilla JavaScript. With reduced motion turned on, every skeleton holds still and each swap is a 200 ms fade.
